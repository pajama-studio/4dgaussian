One pixel has many explanations一个像素有很多种解释

Diffuse observations contain the product ρE. Doubling reflectance and halving illumination leaves a pixel unchanged, within valid material ranges. More camera views constrain geometry but do not automatically resolve all material-light ambiguity under the same illumination.漫反射观测包含乘积 ρE。在合法材质范围内，反射率加倍、光照减半，像素保持不变。更多相机视角约束几何，但在同一光照下不会自动消除全部材质与光照歧义。

At cosine 1, Lambert radiance 0.24 can come from ρ=0.3 and E=0.8π, or ρ=0.6 and E=0.4π. Zero loss on that pixel proves neither material estimate correct.余弦为 1 时，Lambert 辐亮度 0.24 可以来自 ρ=0.3、E=0.8π，也可以来自 ρ=0.6、E=0.4π。单个像素损失为零，不能证明材质估计正确。

One training iteration, end to end一轮训练从头到尾

1. Pick a calibrated camera, time and reference image; linearize the image with a known or modeled camera response. 2. Evaluate canonical Gaussians and deformation. 3. Project geometry and obtain normals/visibility. 4. Evaluate BRDF under estimated light and composite the image.1. 选标定相机、时间与参考图，用已知或建模的相机响应线性化图像。2. 求值 canonical Gaussians 与形变。3. 投影几何并取得法线／可见性。4. 在估计光照下计算 BRDF 并合成图像。

5. Compare predicted pixels to observations; add geometry, material and temporal constraints. 6. Backpropagate through differentiable projection, shading and compositing; update selected parameters. 7. Repeat across views/lights; test held-out lighting. Densification must initialize new material attributes consistently.5. 比较预测与观测，加入几何、材质和时间约束。6. 沿可微投影、着色和合成反向传播，更新选定参数。7. 跨视角／光照重复，在未用于训练的光照下评估。增密时必须一致地初始化新点的材质属性。

Constrain the problem before scaling it up先约束问题，再扩大规模

Known lights, calibrated exposure, multiple illumination conditions, reliable geometry/normals and material priors constrain different ambiguities. Begin with a synthetic object whose cameras, lights and materials are known. Then try real scenes.已知光源、曝光标定、多光照条件、可靠几何／法线与材质先验，分别约束不同歧义。先从相机、光照、材质都已知的合成物体开始，再尝试真实场景。

GS-IR and Relightable 3D Gaussian provide released training code. Their CUDA rasterizers, datasets, checkpoints and exports are separate from this viewer. This site links them but does not claim a reproduced training run. Our implementation is the known-material forward renderer.GS-IR 与 Relightable 3D Gaussian 提供训练代码。它们的 CUDA rasterizer、数据、checkpoint 和导出独立于当前 Viewer。本站提供链接，但不声称复现了训练。我们实现的是已知材质的前向渲染器。
