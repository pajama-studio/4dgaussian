Direct lighting only. No cast shadows, environment map or inverse training. The object is an analytic known-material surface; these are not estimated parameters of the STG research scene. Five modes inspect separate terms, not five independently trained models.仅直射光，没有投射阴影、环境贴图或逆向训练。物体是材质已知的解析曲面，不是对 STG 研究场景估计出的参数。五种模式查看不同项，不是五个独立训练的模型。
